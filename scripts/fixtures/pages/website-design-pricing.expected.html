Pricing · Websites and software

What a website
build costs.

Not every website is the same job, so we don't price it like one. A marketing site, an ecommerce storefront, and a custom software build have different scopes, timelines, and teams behind them — here's how each one is priced.

Scope a website project →
Marketing and brand websites

Marketing and
brand websites.

Priced as a fixed project based on page count, custom design needs, and whether we're building on a CMS you'll manage yourself or something we maintain for you. This includes SEO foundations from day one — no site leaves our hands without proper technical structure.

Ecommerce (Shopify)

Ecommerce on Shopify.

Scoped around your product catalog complexity, required integrations (Klaviyo, inventory, subscriptions), and whether we're doing a full rebuild or a conversion-focused redesign of an existing store.

Custom software

Custom software.

For CRMs, internal tools, or customer-facing platforms that off-the-shelf software can't handle, we scope and price like a software project: discovery, milestones, and a fixed or time-and-materials structure depending on how well-defined the requirements are going in.

Every website project also includes a hosting and maintenance option once it launches — see our maintenance plans for details.
