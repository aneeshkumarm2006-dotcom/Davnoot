Google Ads
for law firms.

Google Ads can help law firms appear when potential clients are actively searching for legal services. But legal advertising becomes expensive quickly, particularly in competitive practice areas, so campaign structure and keyword selection have to be handled carefully.

A firm may advertise several practice areas, locations, and types of legal service. Separating those campaigns is what makes it possible to control the budget and see which searches are generating relevant enquiries.

Google Ads by practice area

A firm's services cover
very different searches.

Someone looking for a personal injury lawyer has a different need from someone looking for a family lawyer or a business attorney. A single campaign covering every practice area makes it hard to control budgets and impossible to read performance.

The practice areas advertised should reflect the services the firm actually provides, and each one can then run its own keywords, advertisements, and landing page.

Target high-intent legal searches

Not every legal search
becomes a client.

Someone searching for "what is personal injury law" is at a different stage from someone searching for "personal injury lawyer near me". Legal keywords are among the most expensive in Google Ads, which makes the distinction costly to ignore.

Location matters just as much for firms that serve specific cities or regions. Campaigns should focus on the areas where the firm actually accepts clients.

01
Practice area

Which service is this search about?

Practice areas compete for the same budget when they share a campaign, and the most expensive one usually wins by accident rather than by decision.

02
Intent

Are they researching or retaining?

Informational searches are cheap and rarely convert. Retaining searches are expensive and sometimes do. Funding both equally is how legal budgets disappear.

03
Competition and cost

What does this click cost here?

The same keyword can cost several times more in one city than another. Competition and cost per click are part of the targeting decision, not a result of it.

04
Client value

What is a new client worth?

A high cost per click is only high relative to what a matter is worth. That comparison is what decides whether a practice area belongs in the account at all.

Legal PPC management

Structure the account
around the services.

A firm might separate personal injury, family law, criminal defense, and immigration into their own campaigns. Each one then uses relevant keywords, advertisements, and landing pages, which gives the person searching a direct path from their query to the service they need.

01

Keyword research

Identify the searches that match the firm's practice areas and locations, and separate the retaining searches from the informational ones.

02

Campaign structure

One campaign per major practice area, so budgets, keywords, advertisements and landing pages can each be controlled without affecting the others.

03

Ad copy

Advertisements that name the practice area, the areas served, and the consultation options, rather than promoting the firm in general. Any claim about results, experience or fees has to be accurate and supported by the firm.

04

Negative keywords

Legal keywords attract informational queries, student research, job searches, and requests for services the firm does not provide. Reviewing search terms is what turns those into an exclusion list.

05

Location targeting

Campaigns focused on the jurisdictions and regions where the firm actually accepts clients, with the rest excluded rather than merely deprioritised.

06

Ongoing optimization

Search terms, keywords, ads, conversions and spend reviewed on a schedule, with changes made because the data asked for them rather than on a monthly cadence.

Send visitors to the practice area

A family law search should land
on the family law page.

A legal campaign should not send every visitor to the firm's homepage. Someone searching for a family law attorney should reach a family law page; someone searching for a personal injury lawyer should find relevant information immediately.

The path is short when it works: legal search, relevant ad, practice-area page, consultation enquiry. The landing page should explain the service clearly and make it easy for a potential client to contact the firm.

Those same practice-area pages are the ones that earn organic visibility, which is why legal PPC and search engine optimization tend to be worked together rather than in sequence.

Track qualified legal leads

Clicks aren't enough to evaluate
a legal campaign.

Tracking these actions is what shows which campaigns and keywords are generating enquiries rather than generating website traffic. For a law firm it is also worth distinguishing a lead from a qualified lead, because not every enquiry suits the firm's practice areas or jurisdiction.

Rather than focusing only on the cost of a click, the useful questions are how many relevant enquiries are being generated, how many become consultations or cases, and what it costs to generate a qualified lead.

How much do Google Ads for law firms cost

Legal clicks are expensive.
That is not the same as unaffordable.

There is no fixed Google Ads cost for law firms. Advertising costs vary significantly depending on the practice area, location, competition, keyword demand, and campaign targeting.

A firm should weigh the value of acquiring a new client against what it costs to generate a qualified lead. Those two numbers together are a better basis for evaluating a legal PPC campaign than the cost per click on its own.

FAQ

Straight answers on
legal PPC.

Are Google Ads effective for law firms?+
They can be effective when campaigns are focused on relevant practice areas, locations, and high-intent searches. Results depend on competition, budget, targeting, landing pages, and conversion tracking, so the answer for any particular firm comes from reviewing those first.
What keywords should law firms use for Google Ads?+
Keywords should reflect the firm's practice areas and locations. Examples include personal injury lawyer, family lawyer, criminal defense lawyer, and lawyer near me. Which of those are worth funding depends on the firm's services and what a matter is worth.
Should each legal practice area have its own campaign?+
Separating major practice areas makes it easier to manage budgets, keywords, advertisements, and landing pages, and stops the most expensive area from consuming the whole budget. The right structure depends on the firm's size and services.
How can a law firm reduce wasted Google Ads spend?+
Use focused keywords, appropriate geographic targeting, negative keywords, and relevant landing pages. Search-term reviews are what identify the informational, educational, and job searches that legal keywords attract in volume.
How should law firms measure Google Ads performance?+
Track the actions that matter to the firm: calls, consultation requests, case evaluation forms, and other enquiries. Where possible, go beyond the number of leads and evaluate their quality, because a campaign can produce many enquiries and few viable matters.
Do you run legal campaigns in French?+
Yes. In Quebec that is usually necessary rather than optional, and the two audiences search with different words. Separate campaigns keep the reporting readable and let each language carry its own budget.
