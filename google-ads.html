Our approach

Our Google Ads
optimization process.

Four stages from a cold or inherited account to campaigns that scale on their own performance data.

01
Week 1 · Audit & strategy

Find out what's actually true.

Analyze existing campaigns, competitors, keywords, audience targeting, conversion tracking, and opportunities. Most accounts have at least one number that has been wrong for months, and everything downstream of it is a guess.

02
Weeks 2–4 · Campaign build

Launch it properly, once.

Launch optimized Search, Shopping, Display, and Performance Max campaigns with proper tracking and audience segmentation. Structure decided before spend starts, so the data that comes back is readable.

03
Month 2 · Optimization

Let the data pick the winners.

Improve bids, Quality Scores, ad copy, search terms, audiences, and landing page performance using real campaign data. This is where cost per acquisition starts falling rather than plateauing.

04
Ongoing · Scale

Pour budget into what works.

Increase budgets on winning campaigns, expand keyword opportunities, launch new ad variations, and maximize long-term ROI. The account should be cheaper to run in month twelve than it was in month three.

Why hire a Google Ads agency in Montreal?+

What Google Ads campaigns do you manage?+

Are you Google Ads certified?+
Yes. Our specialists follow Google Ads best practices and continuously optimize campaigns using the latest bidding strategies, conversion tracking, and performance insights.
How quickly will I see results?+
Unlike SEO, Google Ads can begin generating traffic and leads almost immediately after campaigns launch. Performance improves over time as campaigns collect data and optimizations are made.
