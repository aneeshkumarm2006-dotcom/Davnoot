Why this matters

SEO-optimized Shopify stores
built for growth.

Launching a Shopify store is only the beginning. To generate consistent revenue, your store has to rank in search engines, deliver a shopping experience people finish, and turn visitors into loyal customers.

As a Shopify SEO agency, we combine ecommerce development, technical SEO, and conversion optimization to build stores that attract more qualified traffic and drive long-term online sales — instead of sitting on page four waiting for paid traffic to rescue them.

The problem

Why Shopify stores
struggle to rank.

Many Shopify stores have quality products but still fail to generate consistent organic traffic, because the SEO foundation was never properly optimized. Ranking on Google takes more than publishing products. It depends on how well your store is structured, how easily search engines can crawl it, and how effectively your pages target customer search intent.

Some of the most common Shopify SEO challenges we help solve:

Collection pages competing for the same keywords and reducing ranking potential
Thin or duplicate product content that limits organic visibility
Poor internal linking between collections, products, and supporting content
Slow page speed caused by heavy themes, third-party apps, and unoptimized assets
Missing or incomplete structured data for products, reviews, and breadcrumbs
Duplicate URLs created by filtering, sorting, and faceted navigation
Ranking losses after Shopify migrations due to incorrect redirects or metadata
Missed opportunities to optimize category and collection pages for high-intent searches

Fixing these technical and on-page problems is most of the job, and it is the work a Shopify SEO agency in Montreal should be doing for you: improving search visibility, attracting more qualified traffic, and building organic growth that compounds instead of leaning on paid advertising to carry every sale.

Built in, not bolted on

Shopify SEO is built into
every store.

Plenty of agencies treat SEO as a phase after launch. We build it into every stage of development instead, because the decisions that decide your rankings are made while the store is being architected, not afterwards.

By the time a store launches, its URL structure, template logic, and internal link graph are already set. If those were an afterthought, fixing them later means rebuilding the theme — which is why so many "SEO retainers" on Shopify stores spend a year working around the store instead of on it.

From clean URL structures and schema markup to optimized collection pages, Core Web Vitals, and internal linking, every decision is made to help your Shopify store rank higher and attract more qualified customers.

FAQ

Honest answers
to hard questions.

Why hire a Shopify SEO agency?+
A Shopify SEO agency combines ecommerce development with search engine optimization to improve rankings, attract qualified traffic, and increase online sales. The difference matters on Shopify specifically, because most of what limits a store's rankings lives in the theme and the architecture, and a consultant who can't touch either can only ever recommend fixes.
Will my Shopify store be SEO optimized?+
Yes. Every Shopify store we build includes technical SEO, structured data, optimized architecture, Core Web Vitals improvements, and search-friendly page structures. It's part of the build, not a separate line item you decide on later.
Can you migrate my existing ecommerce website?+
Yes. We migrate WooCommerce, Magento, Wix, BigCommerce, and custom ecommerce platforms while protecting your existing SEO rankings through proper redirects and technical optimization. The redirect map is built before launch and validated after, which is the step most migrations skip.
Do you support Shopify Plus?+
Yes. We build and optimize Shopify Plus stores, including custom checkout experiences, advanced ecommerce functionality, and enterprise-level integrations. If you're running multiple storefronts or markets, we'll architect them so they don't compete with each other in search.
Do you build bilingual Shopify stores?+
Yes. We develop English and French Shopify stores for businesses across Quebec and Canada, with correct hreflang and a seamless multilingual shopping experience, so each language version ranks in its own market instead of splitting the signal.
