01

Technical SEO audits

Your website needs a solid foundation before anything else can work properly. Our 200-point technical review looks for crawl issues, indexing problems, Core Web Vitals, structured data errors, duplicate content, broken links, sitemap issues, and site architecture problems. You get a clear, prioritized fix list your team can actually use.

02

Keyword strategy

We focus on the searches that matter to your business, not just the keywords with the biggest search volumes. We identify commercial-intent keywords, understand what people are looking for, and map those opportunities to the right pages.

03

On-page optimization

We improve the elements that help both search engines and users understand your pages. That includes content, title tags, meta descriptions, headings, internal links, schema markup, images, and conversion-focused improvements.

04

Authority building

Good rankings need more than good content. We build authority through editorial outreach, digital PR, and relevant industry partnerships. No paid link networks and no risky shortcuts, just genuine opportunities to earn relevant links.

05

Local SEO

Want to reach customers searching in Montreal and the surrounding areas? We optimize your Google Business Profile, local citations, service pages, reviews, local content, and location-based keywords to help your business appear where local customers are searching.

06

Performance reporting

You shouldn't have to guess whether your SEO investment is working. Our monthly reporting covers keyword rankings, organic traffic, conversions, revenue, technical improvements, and completed work, so you can see what is actually moving the business forward.

Our approach

How we move
the needle.

SEO takes time, but the work you do early matters.

We follow four simple phases: understand the site, fix the foundation, build what matters, then double down on what works.

01
Days 1–14 · Audit

Map the entire landscape.

We crawl the site, look at your content and competitors, find opportunities, and identify what's getting in the way of growth. By day 14, you'll know where the biggest opportunities are.

02
Days 15–45 · Foundation

Fix the leaks first.

Slow pages, broken links, indexing problems, poor redirects, and other technical issues quietly cost you traffic every day. We fix those problems before putting more content on top of them.

03
Days 46–90 · Build

Create content that earns attention.

We build cornerstone content around your highest-value topics and support it with relevant internal links and editorial outreach. The goal is content that ranks and actually helps your business.

04
Day 90+ · Compound

Double down on what works.

Once we have enough data, we focus more heavily on the topics, pages, and strategies producing results. Instead of spreading your effort everywhere, we build deeper authority where it matters most.

FAQ

SEO Montreal
FAQs.

Why should I hire an SEO company in Montreal?+
A local SEO company can understand the search landscape and the competition that is relevant to Montreal businesses, while still building strategies that work beyond the local market. Davnoot combines technical SEO, content, local SEO, and authority building to help businesses build sustainable organic visibility.
What do Montreal SEO services include?+
Our Montreal SEO services include technical SEO, keyword research, on-page optimization, content strategy, local SEO, internal linking, authority building, and performance reporting. The exact mix depends on your website, your competition, and your business goals.
How long does SEO take?+
SEO is a long-term investment. The first few months usually focus on fixing technical issues, identifying opportunities, and building the foundation. From there, content, authority, and ongoing optimization compound over time.
Can SEO help generate more leads?+
Yes. The goal isn't simply to rank for more keywords. We focus on the commercially relevant searches that bring qualified visitors to your website, and on turning that organic traffic into leads and revenue.
How do you measure SEO success?+
We track rankings, organic traffic, conversions, revenue, technical improvements, and the other business KPIs that matter to you. That way you can see not only whether rankings are improving, but whether SEO is contributing to business growth.
Do you work with SEO consultants or is this a full agency service?+
Davnoot is a full-service SEO agency, not a solo consultant. You get a senior strategist who owns the strategy and the monthly execution: technical fixes, content, and link building, all under one team. You are not handed a report and left to hire someone else to act on it.
Do you offer SEO services outside downtown Montreal, like the West Island or LaSalle?+
Yes. Our local SEO work covers Montreal and the surrounding boroughs, including the West Island and LaSalle, as well as businesses competing nationally beyond the Montreal market.
Do you offer white-label SEO for agencies?+
Yes. We provide white-label SEO services for agencies and consultants who want to offer SEO to their clients without building an in-house team. Work is delivered under your brand, with the same technical audits, content, and reporting included in our standard engagements.
