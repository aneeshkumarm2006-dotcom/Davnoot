Capabilities

Complete search engine
optimization services.

As a full-service SEO agency in Montreal, we combine technical SEO, keyword research, content optimization, local SEO, authority building, and performance tracking into one integrated strategy.

Our goal isn't simply to improve rankings. It's to increase qualified traffic, conversions, and long-term revenue through expert search engine optimization.

01

Technical audits

Technical SEO forms the foundation of every successful SEO campaign. Our 200-point review identifies and resolves crawl issues, indexing errors, Core Web Vitals problems, structured data issues, duplicate content, broken links, XML sitemap errors, and site architecture challenges that limit search performance. You get a prioritized fix list, not a 60-page PDF nobody reads.

02

Keyword strategy

Our keyword research focuses on how your customers actually search. Instead of chasing high-volume vanity terms, we uncover commercial-intent keywords that drive qualified traffic, generate leads, and support long-term business growth. Each keyword is mapped to the right landing page, so your website targets every stage of the customer journey.

03

On-page optimization

Our on-page SEO services improve every element that influences rankings, including content optimization, title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal linking, schema markup, image optimization, and conversion-focused page improvements. Every optimization is backed by data and aligned with Google's best practices.

04

Authority building

We earn high-quality backlinks through editorial outreach, digital PR, and partnerships with trusted industry websites. Rather than purchasing links or using risky shortcuts, we focus on building long-term domain authority that keeps improving rankings over time.

05

Local SEO

Our local SEO services help businesses dominate Google Maps and local search results. We optimize your Google Business Profile, local citations, service pages, reviews, location-based content, and local keyword targeting so customers find your business when searching nearby. Built for companies targeting Montreal and the surrounding areas.

06

Performance reporting

SEO should be transparent. Every month you'll receive detailed reporting showing keyword rankings, organic traffic, conversions, revenue growth, technical improvements, and completed work. Instead of vanity metrics, we focus on the KPIs that directly impact business growth.

Our approach

How we move
the needle.

Our SEO process follows four structured phases designed to build sustainable organic growth. We start by identifying opportunities, fixing technical issues, creating optimized content, and continuously improving performance based on real search data. The result is higher rankings, increased traffic, and measurable business growth.

SEO is patient capital. The right work in the first 90 days determines what you'll harvest for years.

01
Days 1–14 · Audit

Map the entire landscape.

Technical crawl, content gap analysis, competitor reverse-engineering, and a brutal honest scorecard of what's holding the site back. By day 14, you'll know exactly where the leverage is.

02
Days 15–45 · Foundation

Fix the leaks first.

Technical issues compound. Every broken page, slow load, and bad redirect costs you traffic daily. We patch the foundation before adding floors. No new content until the infrastructure can handle it.

03
Days 46–90 · Build

Ship content that earns links.

Cornerstone content on your highest-intent topics, paired with editorial outreach to publications that actually move authority. Every piece is built to rank, and to convert once it does.

04
Day 90+ · Compound

Double down. Repeat.

Once we have signal on what's working, we go ten times harder on those topics. SEO doesn't reward variety. It rewards depth on the things that earn rankings.
