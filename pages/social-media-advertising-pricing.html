How the pricing
is structured.

Like our Google Ads work, we charge a flat monthly management retainer separate from your ad spend. The fee scales with the number of active campaigns and how much creative production is involved — a DTC brand running weekly creative refreshes needs a different level of support than a service business running a handful of evergreen lead ads.
