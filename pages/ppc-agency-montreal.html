Why it works

PPC management for
Montreal businesses.

Paid search is one of the few channels where a person tells you what they want before you spend anything. The job of a PPC agency is to make sure the budget only meets the searches that are worth meeting, and that everything after the click has somewhere useful to go.

Most of the accounts we take over are not broken in an exotic way. The targeting is too broad, the search terms report has never been read, conversions are either untracked or tracking the wrong action, and every visitor lands on the homepage. Those four things account for the majority of wasted spend we find.

If your paid search is running specifically on Google, the platform-level detail lives on our Google Ads agency Montreal page. This page is about how the account is managed, whichever platforms the budget ends up on.

What our PPC agency does

Six parts of the account,
managed together.

A PPC campaign is not one decision. It is a set of settings that only make sense in relation to each other, which is why we manage them as one thing rather than as a monthly checklist.

01

PPC campaign setup

We build campaigns around your business goals, services, locations, and target customers. That includes campaign structure, keyword selection, match types, ad groups, bidding, budgets, and conversion tracking.

02

Keyword research and targeting

The right keywords matter as much as the ad itself. We identify relevant commercial keywords and separate high-intent searches from terms that are unlikely to produce useful leads, then review search terms regularly to find new opportunities.

03

Google Ads management

Google Ads is a major part of PPC, but simply running campaigns is not enough. We monitor performance and make adjustments to bids, budgets, keywords, ads, and targeting based on the data.

04

Negative keyword management

Irrelevant searches can quickly consume a PPC budget. We use negative keywords to reduce unwanted clicks and keep campaigns focused on searches that are relevant to your business.

05

Conversion tracking

Clicks alone do not tell you whether a campaign is working. We set up and review tracking for form submissions, calls, bookings, and other important enquiries so campaign decisions are based on meaningful results.

06

Optimization and reporting

PPC campaigns need ongoing attention. We review search terms, keyword performance, conversion data, cost per lead, ad performance, and budget allocation to identify what should change. Reports stay focused on the numbers that matter.

Local targeting

PPC for businesses
that serve this city.

Local targeting can be important when your customers are located in Montreal or specific areas around the city. Campaigns can be structured around locations, services, and search intent so your advertising budget is not spread across searches that are outside your target market.

For businesses serving customers across multiple locations, campaigns can also be separated by service area to make performance easier to measure. A Montreal campaign and a Toronto campaign in the same ad group tell you nothing about either one.

Campaigns can be run in English, French, or both. In a bilingual market that is a targeting decision as much as a copywriting one, because the two audiences search with different words and rarely at the same cost.

Clicks are the easiest number in the account to move and the least useful one to manage against. We look at the complete picture instead, because a change that improves one of these metrics usually costs you another.

The goal is not simply to get more traffic. It is to make the available PPC budget work more efficiently, which sometimes means spending less in one place so there is something left for the searches that convert.

Is PPC right for your business

More budget on a broken campaign
just costs more.

PPC can be useful when people are already searching for the products or services you provide. It can also be useful for testing demand, promoting specific services, or generating enquiries while organic search visibility develops.

Before increasing a budget, it is worth checking whether the targeting, landing page, conversion tracking, and campaign structure are working properly. Increasing spend on an inefficient campaign usually increases the cost of the problem rather than solving it.

It also pairs badly with nothing. Paid search buys the demand that already exists this quarter; search engine optimization is what lowers the share of that demand you have to keep paying for, and Meta Ads management is what creates demand from people who were not searching yet.

FAQ

Straight answers
on PPC management.

What does a PPC agency actually manage?+
Campaign structure, keyword selection and match types, ad copy, bidding and budgets, negative keywords, location targeting, landing-page recommendations, and conversion tracking. Those settings interact, so managing one without the others usually moves the problem rather than fixing it.
How much should a Montreal business spend on PPC?+
There is no standard figure. The right budget depends on your industry, the competitiveness of your keywords, the cost per click in your area, how many enquiries you want, and what a new customer is worth to you. Those numbers are a better starting point than what another business in your sector spends.
Can you take over an existing PPC account?+
Yes, and it is the more common situation. We review the existing account first: search terms, campaign structure, negatives, targeting, tracking, and where the budget is actually going. That review is what determines whether the account needs restructuring or just maintenance.
Is PPC better than SEO?+
They answer different questions. PPC can produce enquiries as soon as the campaign is live and stops when the budget does. SEO takes longer and keeps working after the spending stops. Businesses that run both usually use paid search to cover the demand organic visibility has not reached yet.
Do you run campaigns in French as well as English?+
Yes. In a bilingual market the two audiences search with different words, convert at different rates, and often cost different amounts per click, so they are worth separating in the account rather than translating one campaign into the other.
How quickly can a PPC campaign start producing enquiries?+
Ads can be live within days, but the first few weeks are mostly data collection. The useful read comes once there are enough tracked conversions to tell which keywords, ads, and locations are producing enquiries rather than clicks.
