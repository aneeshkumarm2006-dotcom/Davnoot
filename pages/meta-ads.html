Why it works

Meta Ads for
Montreal businesses.

Meta advertising does not wait for a search. It reaches people by where they live, what they are interested in, and what they have already done with the business. That covers two jobs at once: finding new customers, and going back for the ones who left without buying.

We manage Meta Ads campaigns across Facebook and Instagram. Four answers shape the account: who the audience is, what the offer is, which locations it runs in, and what the campaign is meant to do. Change one and the structure changes with it.

For Montreal businesses that means pointing campaigns at the service areas and the customer groups the business can actually sell to, not at the whole island. A wider audience is not a better one.

Meta ad creative

The advertisement has to
say it quickly.

A Meta ad interrupts something. It gets a second or two to land the offer before the thumb moves on. That is why the creative carries more of the result here than the targeting does.

So we run combinations of images, videos, headlines, copy, and calls to action against each other and keep the ones that earn their spend. The same tests catch an ad going stale, which means the refresh happens before the cost per lead announces it.

We build campaigns for lead generation, ecommerce sales, website traffic, local business promotion, retargeting, or brand awareness. Which one you are after, and who you need to reach, decides how the account gets structured.

Either way we count the same four things: leads, bookings, purchases, revenue. Clicks are not on that list.

Process

How a Meta campaign
gets built.

Four stages, in that order. Most people do not call or buy the first time they see a business, which is why stage four holds the retargeting and the optimization and never really closes.

01
Before launch

Audience, offer, objective.

We agree on who the campaign is for, what it is offering them, which locations it covers, and what counts as a result. Everything after this is downstream of those four answers.

02
Setup

Campaigns and audiences.

We build the campaign structure for that objective, then the audiences: location, demographics, interests, website visitors, previous engagement, customer lists, and lookalikes where they make sense.

03
First weeks

Creative testing.

We test images, videos, headlines, copy, and calls to action in combination against one audience. Hold the audience still and the difference in results belongs to the ad, not to who saw it.

04
Ongoing

Retargeting and optimization.

People who visited the website, interacted with an ad, or engaged with the business get a different message from a cold audience. We decide what runs next from the tracked numbers.

FAQ

Straight answers
on Meta Ads.

What are Meta Ads?+
Meta Ads are the paid ads that run across Meta's platforms, mainly Facebook and Instagram. You use them to put a product, a service, or an offer in front of a defined audience.
Are Meta Ads good for local businesses?+
Yes, as long as the campaign is pointed at the locations and the audiences the business actually serves. Set it wider than that and you pay to reach people who cannot buy from you.
What is the difference between Meta Ads and Google Ads?+
Google Ads reaches people who are already searching for a product or service. Meta Ads reaches people who are not searching, based on who they are and how they have interacted with Meta platforms.
How do you measure Meta Ads?+
By leads, purchases, and conversions, and by what each one costs: cost per lead, cost per acquisition, revenue, and return on ad spend. Which number leads the report depends on what the campaign was built to do.
