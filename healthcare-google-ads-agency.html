Google Ads for healthcare businesses

Not a standard
local campaign.

A clinic is not a shop with a longer sales cycle. The same levers exist in both accounts and they need different answers in each. Build a practice's account the way you would build a local contractor's and the budget lands in the wrong places.

Our part is narrower than it sounds. Point the budget at searches for the services the practice actually provides, and make the advertisement say the same thing the landing page says. Neither is difficult. Both get skipped often enough that they are the first two things we check.

Match types, bidding, tracking setup and how the account gets reviewed month to month all live on our Google Ads management page. This page is about what changes once the advertiser is a clinic.

What we manage on a healthcare account

Six parts of the account,
managed together.

A healthcare campaign is not one decision. Change the keyword list and the landing page has to change with it. Tighten the targeting and the budget frees up somewhere else. The six below get handled as one account rather than as a monthly checklist of separate tasks.

01

Campaign structure

Campaigns are organised around specific services rather than sending every search to one general page. Each one gets its own keywords, advertisements, landing page, and conversion goals. That is also what makes performance readable per service instead of as one blended average.

02

Keyword research

Research focuses on relevant service, treatment, location, and appointment-related searches, and excludes terms that are unlikely to produce useful enquiries. Someone reading about a condition and someone looking for a provider are not the same audience, even when they type similar words.

03

Location targeting

Campaigns focus on the cities or areas where the practice accepts patients rather than spending budget on searches from locations outside its service area. Location-specific ad groups and landing pages are used where the practice serves more than one area.

04

Conversion tracking

Appointment requests, phone calls, consultation requests, contact forms, and booking enquiries are tracked as separate actions. Tracking is what distinguishes people who clicked an advertisement from people who actually contacted the practice.

05

Landing page relevance

The click lands on a page about the service that was searched for. Before the campaign starts funding that page, we check it names the service, the location, and the next step.

06

Ongoing optimization

Search terms, advertisements, locations, and budgets are adjusted based on performance. That review is continuous rather than a launch task, because new irrelevant searches arrive every month whether anyone reads them or not.

not one for everything.

Healthcare keyword research

The search says how close
someone is to booking.

Healthcare searches vary wildly in intent. Someone looking for a specific treatment or a local clinic is close to contacting a provider. Someone reading up on general health information is not. In a keyword tool the two sit side by side looking identical.

So research covers service, treatment, location, and appointment-related searches, and cuts the terms unlikely to produce a useful enquiry. The second half of that job is where most of a healthcare budget gets saved.

Symptom and condition reading, home remedies, training and course searches, and job searches all sit close to clinical keywords. They arrive on their own if nothing stops them, which is why the search terms report gets read before any bid is touched.

Local healthcare advertising

Advertise where the practice
accepts patients.

A clinic can only treat people who can get to it. So the campaign is set to the cities and areas where the practice accepts patients, and every click from outside them is money spent on someone who will book closer to home.

Where a practice runs more than one site, each site gets its own ad groups and landing pages. Otherwise the two get averaged, and the average hides which location is actually producing enquiries.

Campaigns can run in English, French, or both. In a bilingual market that is a targeting decision as much as a copywriting one, because the two audiences search with different words and rarely at the same cost.

Healthcare lead generation

A click is not
an enquiry.

The conversions on a healthcare account are appointment requests, phone calls, consultation requests, contact forms, and booking enquiries. Which of them matters most follows from how the practice takes patients, so we settle that before anything gets configured.

Conversion tracking separates the people who clicked an advertisement from the people who contacted the practice. Without it the account reports traffic, the front desk reports a quiet phone, and neither number explains the other.

Tracked separately, these actions make cost per lead legible per service. Once an appointment request from one service line has a cost and a consultation request from another has a different one, deciding where the budget goes stops being a matter of opinion.

Google Ads optimization

An account can have plenty of clicks
and still book nobody.

Campaign performance is reviewed across the numbers below rather than against a single headline figure. A change that improves one of them usually costs you another, so they are read together, and the search terms report is read first.

Search terms, advertisements, locations, and budgets then get adjusted on what those numbers show. Every one of those changes is aimed at the same thing: putting the available budget in front of more of the searches that end in an appointment. Traffic to the clinic website is beside the point.

Landing pages for healthcare ads

A service search should land
on the service page.

An advertisement for a specific physiotherapy or diagnostic service points at that service page, not at the homepage. Whatever the search was about, the page the click lands on has to be about the same thing.

That page has to answer three things without being clicked through: what the service is, where it is offered, and how to book it. A visitor who has to work through a menu to confirm the clinic even offers the thing they searched for usually does not bother.

Where those service pages do not exist yet, that is a website problem before it is an advertising one. Worth fixing before more budget runs through it.

FAQ

Straight answers on
healthcare Google Ads.

Can healthcare businesses use Google Ads?+
Yes. Healthcare businesses can use Google Ads to advertise eligible services, subject to Google's advertising policies and applicable regulations. Which services qualify, and how they can be described, is worth confirming before the campaign is built rather than after an advertisement is disapproved.
What keywords should healthcare businesses target?+
Keywords should relate directly to the services offered, the locations served, and searches that indicate genuine interest in contacting the practice. General health information searches look similar in a keyword tool and behave nothing alike once the budget is live.
Can Google Ads target patients in a specific location?+
Yes. Campaigns can be configured to target relevant cities, regions, or service areas, and to exclude the places a click can never turn into a visit. Location-specific ad groups and landing pages can be used where a practice serves more than one area.
How do you measure healthcare Google Ads?+
On calls, appointment requests, consultation enquiries, contact forms, cost per lead, and conversion rate. Which one the campaign is judged on depends on how the practice takes patients. Clicks and impressions are context for those numbers, not a substitute for them.
