The shift

What Is Answer Engine
Optimization?

Search is changing. Instead of browsing ten blue links, users increasingly rely on AI-powered search engines that provide direct answers.

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) helps your business become one of those answers by structuring your website so AI platforms can understand, trust, and cite your content.

Unlike traditional SEO that focuses on rankings, AEO focuses on earning citations and visibility inside AI-generated responses.

Services

Complete AEO services.

Instead of simply improving rankings, our AEO service helps businesses build long-term visibility across both traditional search engines and AI-powered answer engines.

01

AI content structure

Answers moved to the top of the page: clear definitions, question-led headings, and self-contained passages an engine can lift without guessing at context.

02

Schema and entity SEO

Organization, Service, FAQ and Article markup, plus consistent entity signals, so engines know exactly who you are and what you do.

03

Topical authority

Clusters, internal linking and E-E-A-T signals that make your site the obvious source on a subject rather than one more page about it.

04

Citation building

Getting your brand mentioned, listed and linked on the third-party sources that AI engines already read and quote in your category.

05

GEO optimization

Generative Engine Optimization across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot. Each one retrieves and summarizes differently, so each one gets tuned.

06

AI search monitoring

Prompt-level tracking of where you appear, which sources beat you to the citation, and what actually changed since last month.

What is Answer Engine Optimization?+
Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the process of optimizing your website so AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Gemini can understand, trust, and reference your content.
What's the difference between SEO and AEO?+
SEO focuses on improving rankings in traditional search results, while AEO helps your business become a direct answer in AI-generated search experiences.
Do you optimize for ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews?+
Yes. We optimize content for ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini, and other emerging AI search platforms using structured content, schema markup, entity optimization, and authoritative content strategies.
Is AEO different from GEO?+
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) focuses on helping content become a trusted answer, while GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) broadens that strategy across generative AI systems. We combine both approaches to maximize AI search visibility.
Do you offer bilingual AEO services?+
Yes. We create and optimize English and French content to help Montreal businesses improve AI search visibility across bilingual audiences and local markets.
