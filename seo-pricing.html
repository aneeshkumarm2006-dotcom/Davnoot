What SEO
actually costs.

Every agency gets asked this question first, and most dodge it with a "it depends" and a sales call. We'll give you the real answer: SEO pricing depends on how competitive your market is, how much technical work your site needs, and how fast you want to move. Here's how we actually price it.

What drives the cost

What drives
the cost.

Competitiveness of your market
A Montreal law firm competing for "personal injury lawyer" costs more to rank than a niche regional service.
Site health
A site with years of technical debt needs more work upfront than a clean, modern build.
Content gap
How much you're missing compared to competitors already ranking.
Speed
A 6-month sprint costs differently than a 12-month compounding plan.
How we structure engagements

How we structure
an engagement.

We run SEO as a monthly retainer, not a one-time project — rankings compound, and so should the work. Most engagements start with a paid audit and 90-day roadmap, then move into ongoing execution: technical fixes, content, link building, and increasingly, AI search optimization so you show up in ChatGPT and Gemini answers, not just Google's blue links.

We don't publish a fixed price list because doing so means either overcharging simple projects or underdelivering on complex ones. What we will do is give you a specific number after we've looked at your actual site and market — usually within a day of your audit call.
