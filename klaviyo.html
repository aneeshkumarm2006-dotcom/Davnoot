Why Klaviyo

Klaviyo isn't just
an email platform.

It combines customer data, email marketing, SMS marketing, and real-time behavioral automation into one platform that helps ecommerce brands build stronger customer relationships and generate more repeat revenue.

That's also why most accounts underperform. The platform will happily run on default flows and a single "all subscribers" list, and it will still send. It just won't earn much.

As a Klaviyo Partner Agency, we help businesses unlock the platform's full potential through advanced automation, segmentation, personalization, and continuous optimization.

Capabilities

Complete Klaviyo email
marketing services.

Everything from account setup and migration to automated flows, segmentation, SMS, and revenue reporting.

Every part of it is built around what your customers actually do, not around a send calendar.

01

Klaviyo setup and migration

Account configuration, list migration, domain authentication, and platform integrations. Sending domains and consent records get handled properly at the start, because a warmed reputation is much harder to rebuild than to protect.

02

Email automation flows

Welcome series, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post purchase, win back, replenishment, VIP, back in stock, and sunset. Built once, then optimized every month, earning revenue between your campaigns.

03

Advanced segmentation

Behavioral segments, purchase history, predicted lifetime value, customer engagement, dynamic lists, and VIP customers. Your best buyers and your discount hunters should never receive the same email.

04

Personalized campaigns

Targeted email campaigns based on customer behavior instead of sending the same message to every subscriber. Fewer sends, aimed better, is usually what fixes both revenue and unsubscribe rate at once.

05

SMS marketing

Klaviyo SMS campaigns, SMS automation, cart reminders, flash sale campaigns, and VIP notifications. Used where a text genuinely beats an email, and quiet everywhere else.

06

Reporting and optimization

Revenue attribution, flow optimization, A/B testing, deliverability, and customer lifetime value. Reported against orders in your store, not against opens.

The nine flows we build and maintain inside every Klaviyo account. Each one is triggered by something a customer did, which is why they keep producing long after the campaign calendar runs out.

Process

Our Klaviyo
process.

Four stages, run continuously. We audit what already exists, build what's missing, optimize what works, then scale it.

01
Audit

Find the revenue already in the account.

Review your Klaviyo account, customer journeys, segmentation, deliverability, and revenue performance. Most accounts are leaking somewhere obvious: a welcome flow with two emails, a cart flow that fires too late, or a list that has never been sunset.

02
Build

Automations built on real behavior.

Create personalized email and SMS automations based on real-time customer behavior. Flows first, because they run whether or not anyone remembers to schedule a campaign that week.

03
Optimize

Improve every variable that matters.

Continuously improve flows, campaigns, segmentation, and messaging to increase conversions and repeat purchases. Every test is documented, so the account gets smarter instead of just busier.

04
Scale

Grow what's already working.

Expand successful automations while improving customer lifetime value and ecommerce revenue. This is where SMS, predictive segments, and deeper personalization start paying for themselves.

Why hire a Klaviyo Partner Agency?+
A Klaviyo Partner Agency helps businesses maximize the platform through advanced automation, segmentation, email marketing, SMS campaigns, and ongoing optimization that improves customer retention and revenue.
Do you build Klaviyo automation flows?+
Yes. We create welcome series, abandoned cart flows, browse abandonment, post-purchase journeys, replenishment reminders, win-back campaigns, and other behavioral automations.
Do you manage Klaviyo SMS?+
Yes. We build integrated email and SMS programs using Klaviyo to create a consistent customer experience across multiple communication channels.

Yes. We migrate businesses from Mailchimp, Omnisend, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, and other email platforms into Klaviyo while preserving customer data and automations.
